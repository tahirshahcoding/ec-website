# Design Guidelines

The Team section should feel premium and modern.

Avoid traditional employee cards.

Instead:

- Large professional portraits
- Minimal typography
- Interactive hover effects
- Smooth motion
- Short biographies
- Skill tags
- Social links (optional)

Each member should have a dedicated profile card with subtle animations that reflect EagleNest Creations' premium design language.
